Neue Montreal (Pangram Pangram) is a licensed font, so it is not bundled.
Put these three files here (exact names) and the site picks them up automatically:

  NeueMontreal-Regular.woff2   (400)
  NeueMontreal-Medium.woff2    (500)
  NeueMontreal-Bold.woff2      (700, also used for the 800 headings)

Convert the .otf/.ttf you bought to .woff2 if needed (e.g. with fonttools or transfonter.org).
